Methodology & limitations

Frequency counts distinct feedback records; themes can overlap. Severity: 1 mild, 2 friction, 3 critical. Priority = 35% frequency + 30% severity + 20% curated commercial relevance + 15% segment size, each normalized to 0–100. Segment size uses distinct customer IDs in the largest affected segment. Commercial relevance is an assumption, not measured revenue.

  • English lexicon baseline, not a trained sentiment model
  • Curated problem and campaign templates, not verified product claims
  • Synthetic data and sampling bias prevent market-wide conclusions
  • Customers may appear in multiple segments; sizes are distinct IDs per segment
