## Methodology and limitations

Frequency counts distinct feedback records, not customers. Themes can overlap. Negative sentiment is a proxy for pain; questions and concerns are objection candidates.
Priority = 35% frequency + 30% severity + 20% commercial relevance + 15% segment size. Components are normalized to 0–100; severity uses a 1–3 scale. Commercial relevance is a curated assumption, not revenue evidence. Segment size counts distinct customer IDs in the largest affected segment.

- English lexicon baseline, not a trained sentiment model
- Curated problem and campaign templates, not verified product claims
- Synthetic data and sampling bias prevent market-wide conclusions
- Customers may appear in multiple segments; sizes are distinct IDs per segment
